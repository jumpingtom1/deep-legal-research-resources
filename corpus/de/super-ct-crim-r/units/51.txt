Rule 51. Exceptions unnecessary.
Exceptions to rulings or orders of the court are unnecessary and for all purposes for which an exception
has heretofore been necessary it is sufficient that a party, at the time the ruling or order of the court is
made or sought, makes known to the court the action which that party desires the court to take or that
party’s objection to the action of the court and the grounds therefor; but if a party has no opportunity to
object to a ruling or order, the absence of an objection does not thereafter prejudice that party.
History.
Adopted, effective Feb. 12, 1953; revised, effective Jan. 1, 1992.
