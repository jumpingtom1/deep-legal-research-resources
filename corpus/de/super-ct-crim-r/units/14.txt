Rule 14. Relief from prejudicial joinder.
If it appears that a defendant or the state is prejudiced by a joinder of offenses or of defendants in an
indictment or information or by such joinder for trial together, the court may order an election or separate
trials of counts, grant a severance of defendants or provide whatever other relief justice requires. In ruling
on a motion by a defendant for severance the court may order the attorney general to deliver to the court
for inspection in camera any statements or confessions made by the defendants which the state intends to
introduce in evidence at the trial.
History.
Adopted, effective Feb. 12, 1953; amended, effective June 1, 1969; revised, effective Jan. 1, 1992.
