# Build order for the remaining jurisdictions

There are 46 jurisdictions to build. California, Delaware and New York are
done. Federal, Oregon and Washington stay bundled in the skill.

**How the order was chosen:** expected demand from an Oregon-based practice.
That means large-state litigation volume, business-law importance, and
Oregon's western neighbors, roughly in that order. Smaller states are grouped
into later waves.

**How each wave runs:** six jurisdictions are researched in parallel. Each
one is then checked independently from its generated instructions alone, the
check's findings are fixed, and the wave is committed.
